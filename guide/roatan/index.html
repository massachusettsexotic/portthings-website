Roatán

Honduras

A deep, offline guide to Roatán, inside the Port Things app. Every place shows walking minutes from your pier, and the day plans are timed around the all-aboard. Every place, description and day plan is on the phone, so it works with no signal. Only the map background needs one.

Where the ships dock

  • Town Center pier, Coxen Hole
  • Mahogany Bay cruise pier

Ships usually dock ~7–8 AM and sail ~4–5 PM · Honduras keeps Central Standard Time (UTC−6) all year — check whether your ship keeps a different time

Ready-made days

  • West Bay & West End

    From the Town Center pier in Coxen Hole: snorkel West Bay, water taxi to West End, Half Moon Bay, back in good time

  • Gumbalimba & West Bay

    From the Mahogany Bay pier, the long run west: monkeys and macaws, then the island's famous beach

  • Carambola & Sandy Bay

    Carambola's garden trail, the marine institute at Anthony's Key, lunch in West End

  • An Easy Mahogany Day

    No taxi, no traffic: the chairlift, the beach and the shops, steps from the ship

  • Iguanas & the Keys

    A short hop east from Mahogany Bay: the iguana sanctuary, then an afternoon on a cay

  • Punta Gorda & Oak Ridge

    The Garifuna community of 1797, lunch in the village and a boat through the mangroves — a long day

  • Half Moon Bay Snorkel

    From the Town Center pier: the reef at West End's little bay, in 4 hours

  • Barrier Reef by Boat

    A morning snorkel or dive with a West End operator, back by lunchtime

  • Zip Line by the Pier

    A canopy tour in the hills above Coxen Hole — the shortest adventure

  • Maya Key with Kids

    A small island park by boat: beach, pool, snorkelling and rescued animals

  • Gardens & Butterflies

    A garden trail to a reef view, then a butterfly garden — back by 1 PM

  • Iguanas, Monkeys & Sloths

    French Cay's animal parks, 20 minutes from Mahogany Bay

  • Little French Key Morning

    A cay a short boat hop from French Harbour: beach and pools

  • Rainy Day at Mahogany Bay

    Stay inside the gate: covered shops, gelato and no traffic

  • Punta Gorda in a Morning

    One of Honduras's first Garifuna communities and a Garifuna lunch, in 4 hours

  • Oak Ridge Mangroves by Boat

    Stilt houses, mangrove tunnels and a slow lunch over the water

  • Camp Bay & the Quiet East

    Long port days only: an empty beach behind the reef at the far east end
