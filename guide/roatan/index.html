Arrival day: the essentials

Whether you have a port day or a week, the first hour sets it up. Nine things worth knowing, roughly in order.

  1. 1

    Know which pier you're at

    Ships dock either at Mahogany Bay, Carnival's cruise center at Dixon Cove, or at the Port of Roatán's Town Center pier in Coxen Hole, about 5 km west. Every distance in this app is measured from the pier nearer that place, and the pier is named — check your ship's port sheet.

  2. 2

    Taxis: agree the fare before you get in

    Taxis aren't metered. Ask the price to your stop, whether it's per person or for the whole car, one way or round trip, and in dollars or lempiras — then agree before you sit down. If the driver will wait or come back, agree the time too. White shared taxis ('colectivos') run fixed routes cheaply, but the US State Department advises against public transport in Honduras.

  3. 3

    Island time vs. ship time

    Honduras keeps Central Standard Time (UTC−6) all year, with no daylight saving. Your ship may keep a different clock — all-aboard is always on SHIP time. West Bay is the far end of the island from Mahogany Bay: leave it at least 2½ hours before all-aboard.

  4. 4

    Dollars, lempiras and small bills

    US dollars are accepted almost everywhere tourists go, but change often comes in lempiras (about L26–27 to US$1 in September 2026). Carry small US bills for taxis, water taxis and tips. Cards work in resorts and bigger restaurants; ATMs are in Coxen Hole, French Harbour, West End and West Bay.

  5. 5

    Your phone is roaming

    Honduras isn't covered by most US plans at home rates — check your plan's international day pass before you land. Turn off cellular data at sea: the ship's network charges separately. Offline maps and this guide work with no signal.

Ready-made days

  • West Bay & West End

    From the Town Center pier in Coxen Hole: snorkel West Bay, water taxi to West End, Half Moon Bay, back in good time

  • Gumbalimba & West Bay

    From the Mahogany Bay pier, the long run west: monkeys and macaws, then the island's famous beach

  • Carambola & Sandy Bay

    Carambola's garden trail, the marine institute at Anthony's Key, lunch in West End

  • An Easy Mahogany Day

    No taxi, no traffic: the chairlift, the beach and the shops, steps from the ship

  • Iguanas & the Keys

    A short hop east from Mahogany Bay: the iguana sanctuary, then an afternoon on a cay

  • Punta Gorda & Oak Ridge

    The Garifuna community of 1797, lunch in the village and a boat through the mangroves — a long day

  • Half Moon Bay Snorkel

    From the Town Center pier: the reef at West End's little bay, in 4 hours

  • Barrier Reef by Boat

    A morning snorkel or dive with a West End operator, back by lunchtime

  • Zip Line by the Pier

    A canopy tour in the hills above Coxen Hole — the shortest adventure

  • Maya Key with Kids

    A small island park by boat: beach, pool, snorkelling and rescued animals

  • Gardens & Butterflies

    A garden trail to a reef view, then a butterfly garden — back by 1 PM

  • Iguanas, Monkeys & Sloths

    French Cay's animal parks, 20 minutes from Mahogany Bay

  • Little French Key Morning

    A cay a short boat hop from French Harbour: beach and pools

  • Rainy Day at Mahogany Bay

    Stay inside the gate: covered shops, gelato and no traffic

  • Punta Gorda in a Morning

    One of Honduras's first Garifuna communities and a Garifuna lunch, in 4 hours

  • Oak Ridge Mangroves by Boat

    Stilt houses, mangrove tunnels and a slow lunch over the water

  • Camp Bay & the Quiet East

    Long port days only: an empty beach behind the reef at the far east end
