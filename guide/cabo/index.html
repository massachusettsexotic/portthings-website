    A surf lesson, the Hotel California, and a Pueblo Mágico

  • East Cape & Cabo Pulmo

    The living reef, a shady beach and the kite town

    Mexico's most beautiful beach, then the Malecón at sunset — a long day

    Sol de Mayo, the oasis town of Santiago and the leather village

    Water taxi past the Arch and sea lions to Lover's Beach, lunch on Médano, back by 1 PM

    A swim facing the Arch and lunch on the sand, back by 1 PM

  • Snorkel Boat: Pelican Rock & Santa María

  • Humpbacks from the Marina

  • Rainy Day in Cabo San Lucas

  • Lover's Beach at First Light

    The first water taxi to an empty beach, then sunset on the Pacific side

  • Sea Lions at Espíritu Santo

    A long day from La Paz: snorkel with the pups, a beach lunch and the Malecón sunset

  • Ziplines & the Old Lighthouse

    A canyon adventure park, an ATV or horseback ride to Cabo Falso, and sunset at Solmar

  • A First Surf Lesson

    Old Man's gentle wave in the morning, Costa Azul's surfers at lunch, a calm swim at Palmilla

  • San José for Free

    For hotel-zone guests: birds at the estuary, the mission, the plaza and the gallery streets
