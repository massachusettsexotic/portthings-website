Puerto Vallarta

Mexico

A deep, offline guide to Puerto Vallarta, inside the Port Things app. Every place shows walking minutes from your pier, and the day plans are timed around the all-aboard. Every place, description and day plan is on the phone, so it works with no signal. Only the map background needs one.

Ready-made days

  • The Malecón & El Centro

    The seafront sculptures, the arches, the church, the river island and market

  • Los Muertos Beach Day

    The Zona Romántica's beach, lunch on the sand and the pier

  • El Centro, the Easy Way

    The flat square and promenade, the arches and the church — back in under 4 hours

  • Snorkelling at Los Arcos

    A panga from Mismaloya to the sea arches, then lunch on the cove

  • Yelapa by Water Taxi

    Long port days only: a village with no roads, its beach and its waterfall

  • Las Ánimas & Quimixto

    A water taxi from Boca de Tomatlán to two boat-only beaches

  • The Trail to Colomitos

    A rocky jungle trail from Boca de Tomatlán to a tiny green cove

  • Vallarta Botanical Garden

    Orchids and forest trails in the hills, then the fishing village at Boca

  • The Marietas Islands

    A permitted boat tour: snorkelling, seabirds and — if allowed — the Hidden Beach

  • Mangroves & Crocodiles

    The estuary reserve behind the marina, minutes from the terminal

  • Vallarta for (Almost) Free

    The local bus into town, the free promenade, the church and street tacos

  • Rainy Day in El Centro

    The naval museum, the chocolate museum and a long Mexican lunch

  • A Taco Crawl

    Market kitchens, tacos al pastor and fish tacos in the Zona Romántica

  • The Conchas Chinas Coves

    Quiet coves and tide pools south of town, lunch by the pier

  • Bucerías for the Day

    A long beach, cobbled lanes of galleries and seafood in Nayarit

  • Punta de Mita

    A surf lesson or a swim at the tip of the bay, and seafood on the sand

  • Sayulita

    A long day to the surf village north of the bay

  • The Zoo & Mismaloya Beach

    A jungle zoo up the valley, then a swim and lunch in the cove

  • The Crocodile Sanctuary

    A rescue sanctuary north of the airport, back in under 4 hours

  • Lighthouse & Hilltop Cross

    Stairs above El Centro to the best views of the bay, then lunch

  • A Marina Stroll

    The flat boardwalk round the yacht harbour next to the terminal
