Ready-made days

  • Canal Ring & Old Centre

    The Dam to the Jordaan on foot — the classic first day

  • Museum Quarter Day

    Rijksmuseum, Van Gogh, Vondelpark, and lunch on the Albert Cuyp

  • Windmills & Waterland

    Zaanse Schans, Marken, Volendam and Edam in one loop

  • Haarlem & the Beach

    The prettier little sister, then the North Sea at Zandvoort

  • Across the Water to Noord

    Free ferries, the EYE, the Lookout and NDSM's street art

  • Harbour & Plantage

    NEMO, the maritime museum, the botanical garden and the zoo

  • Castles & Forts

    Muiderslot, the island fort of Pampus, and Naarden's star ramparts

  • Utrecht Day

    The Dom Tower, wharf cellars and a fairy-tale castle

  • Tulip Day (spring only)

    Keukenhof at opening, the bulb fields by bike, then Leiden

  • Rijksmuseum & Van Gogh on a Rainy Day

    The Night Watch, a free lunchtime concert, Van Gogh and the Stedelijk — a short dash apart

  • The Old Centre on a Rainy Day

    Antiquities, pancakes up a steep stair, a church in an attic, a canal-house home and a cat museum

  • The Jordaan on Foot

    Apple pie in a brown café, the Noordermarkt, Westerpark and Rembrandt's church — free to wander

  • Along the Amstel by Bike

    A windmill on the river, poffertjes in Ouderkerk and the forest park — for confident riders

  • Vondelpark, the Bos & the Goat Farm

    Playgrounds, the city's forest park, pancakes in a farmhouse and kid goats — almost free

  • A Taste of Amsterdam

    A famous cookie, herring and stroopwafels at the market, aged Gouda, a jenever tasting and a food hall

  • Amsterdam for Free

    A hidden courtyard, Dam Square, the free ferry, Eye's terrace, NEMO's roof and the library view

  • Alkmaar Cheese Market

    Friday mornings, April to September: the cheese carriers, the museum in the Waag and the old town

  • Dunes & North Sea by Bike

    Bikes from Haarlem into the dunes to see the bison, lunch on the beach and a swim at Zandvoort

  • The Jewish Cultural Quarter

    One ticket, a few flat minutes apart: the museum, the candle-lit synagogue, the Holocaust museum and the names monument

  • In Rembrandt's Footsteps

    His house and studio, the Anatomy Lesson's Waag, Saskia's church, the Night Watch and his last resting place

  • Canals by Boat, Easy Pace

    An open-boat cruise, lunch in the Nine Streets, the flower market and the Skinny Bridge — little walking
