Ready-made days

  • Dockyard on Foot

    Museum, craft market, glassblowers, the pub and the beach — all inside the walls, all five minutes from the ship

  • South Shore Beaches by Bus 7

    Horseshoe Bay, the walk through the dunes to Jobson's Cove, the lighthouse and the island's best shore snorkel

  • Hamilton by Ferry

    Twenty minutes across the Sound to Front Street, the cathedral tower, the fort, the pub and a shipwreck museum

  • St. George's & the Forts

    The Orange ferry to the 1612 town, St. Peter's, the perfumery, Tobacco Bay and Fort St. Catherine

  • Caves, Zoo & the Swizzle Inn

    The aquarium at Flatts, the Crystal Caves, a jug of swizzle and a swim in the Blue Hole

  • Somerset & the Railway Trail

    The smallest drawbridge, a hilltop fort, a beach that appears at low tide and the West End's fish sandwich

  • St. David's & Clearwater

    The lighthouse, the old NASA station, shark hash at the Black Horse and sea glass under the guns

  • The Dockyard for Free

    On foot from the ship: island art, the craft market, glassblowing and the lagoons

  • Rainy Day at the Dockyard

    The national museum's halls, local art, glassblowing, a pub lunch — steps from the ship

  • Horseshoe Bay Morning

    Bus 7 to the pink crescent and the calm pool at Port Royal Cove, back by 1 PM

  • Snorkel Sail from the Dockyard

    A booked catamaran to the western reefs and wrecks, back by 12:30

  • Hamilton from the Front Street Berth

    For ships in the city: the gallery, the cathedral tower, coffee and a balcony lunch

  • Rainy Day in Hamilton

    By ferry: shipwreck treasure, chowder at the Hog Penny and the National Gallery

  • Hamilton, Flat and Easy

    The ferry, the harbourfront, the Underwater Institute and lunch at the pink hotel

  • St. George's from Ordnance Island

    For small ships in the old town: the square, the museum, St. Peter's and a harbour lunch

  • Tobacco Bay from Ordnance Island

    A walk over the hill to the East End's best snorkel, back by 1 PM

  • Fish Sandwiches, Chowder & Swizzles

    Hamilton by ferry: roasted coffee, Art Mel's famous sandwich, the Hog Penny and a balcony swizzle

  • Kayak to Castle Island

    A guided paddle to the oldest English fort in the New World, then the Blue Hole and a swizzle

  • Gardens, Homer & Elbow Beach

    A slow day: the Botanical Gardens, Winslow Homer's watercolours, pink sand and a harbour drink

  • Spittal Pond & John Smith's Bay

    A clifftop nature walk, a calm pink beach and an untouched 1710 mansion
