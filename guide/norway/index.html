Norway's Fjords

Norway

A deep, offline guide to Norway's Fjords, inside the Port Things app. Every place shows walking minutes from your pier, and the day plans are timed around the all-aboard. Every place, description and day plan is on the phone, so it works with no signal. Only the map background needs one.

Where the ships dock

  • Ålesund: Storneskaia / Skansekaia
  • Alta: Alta harbour, Bossekop
  • Åndalsnes & Romsdal: Åndalsnes cruise quay
  • Bergen: Skolten / Bontelabo (Jekteviken & Dokken for some ships)
  • Bodø & Saltstraumen: Bodø city quay (beside the Hurtigruten terminal)
  • Eidfjord & Hardanger: Eidfjord cruise pier
  • Flåm & the Aurlandsfjord: Flåm cruise quay (tenders on multi-ship days)
  • Geiranger: Geiranger tender quay / Seawalk pier
  • Haugesund & Avaldsnes: Garpaskjærskaien
  • Honningsvåg & the North Cape: Honningsvåg harbour quay (tenders when two ships call)
  • Kirkenes: Kirkenes harbour quay (industrial quay with shuttle for some ships)
  • Kristiansand: Vestre Havn cruise quay (Kongsgård for the largest ships)
  • Lofoten: Svolvær harbour quay (Leknes for large ships; tenders on busy days)
  • Molde & the Atlantic Road: Storkaia
  • Olden & Loen: Olden cruise quay
  • Oslo: Søndre Akershuskai / Revierkaia / Vippetangen (Filipstad for some ships)
  • Stavanger & the Lysefjord: Vågen — Strandkaien & Skagenkaien
  • Tromsø: Prostneset (Breivika for large ships, 4 km north, shuttle)
  • Trondheim: Pier 68 / Brattøra (Kai 2 by Rockheim for some ships)

Ready-made days

  • Bergen: Bryggen, Fløyen & the Fish Market

    The wharf, the funicular, a market lunch and the best art on the coast — all on foot from the pier

  • Stavanger & the Lysefjord

    The old town, the oil museum and three hours under Pulpit Rock by boat

  • Haugesund & the Vikings

    The pink town hall, Harald Fairhair's grave and the royal seat at Avaldsnes

  • Flåm: Train, Fjord & Vikings

    Up the mountain by rail, down the Nærøyfjord by boat, and a Viking beer at the end

  • Eidfjord & Vøringsfossen

    The plateau's edge, the step bridge over the waterfall and an Iron Age hillside

  • Olden: Glacier & Skylift

    Briksdal's blue ice in the morning and a thousand metres up Mount Hoven after lunch

  • Geiranger: Three Viewpoints & the Fjord

    The Fjord Centre, Flydalsjuvet, the Eagle Bend and ninety minutes under the Seven Sisters

  • Ålesund: Art Nouveau & Aksla

    The Jugendstil streets, 418 steps to the view, bacalao on the canal and the aquarium in the rocks

  • Oslo: Fortress, Polar Ships & The Scream

    Akershus from the gangway, the Bygdøy ferry to the Fram and Kon-Tiki, lunch on the waterfront, Munch at the National Museum and the Opera roof on the way back

  • Kristiansand: Old Town, Zoo & the City Beach

    The white wooden streets of Posebyen before breakfast, three hours at Dyreparken with the wolves and the pirates, shrimp on the fish-market pontoon and a swim at Bystranda

  • Trondheim: Nidaros, the Old Bridge & Bakklandet

    Scandinavia's great cathedral and the crown jewels, the red bridge to the wooden quarter, fish soup and the bicycle lift, the fortress view and the rock museum by the pier

  • Åndalsnes: Gondola, Trollstigen & the Troll Wall

    Eight minutes up to Nesaksla and the platform over the valley, the bus up the eleven hairpins, lunch at the climbing centre and Europe's tallest rock face before all-aboard

  • Molde: 222 Peaks & the Atlantic Road

    The Varden panorama while the morning is clear, the folk museum on the hill, and the afternoon out to the bridges that leap across the open sea

  • Tromsø: Arctic Cathedral, Cable Car & the Brewery

    The polar museum on the quay, over the bridge to the white triangles and the view from Storsteinen, seals at 12:30, a Mack at the world's northernmost brewery pub

  • Honningsvåg: The North Cape & the King Crab

    The bus to the top of Europe for the globe, the cliff and the film in the rock, then a RIB out to the crab pots and a feast on the shore, and the church that survived 1944

  • Alta: Rock Carvings, the Aurora Cathedral & the River

    Six thousand figures on the shore rock at Hjemmeluft, the titanium spiral in the centre, and lunch up the salmon river at the lodge that becomes an ice hotel each winter

  • Lofoten: Trollfjord, Sea Eagles & Henningsvær

    The boat into the 100-metre fjord with eagles off the stern, twenty minutes in the ice gallery, the village on the islets with the football pitch on the rocks, and the little war museum by the ship

  • Bodø: Saltstraumen & the Aviation Museum

    Check the tide, then the bus to the strongest current on earth, the U-2 and the Spitfire in the propeller building, and the cairn above town with the Lofoten wall on the horizon

  • Kirkenes: The Russian Border & the Bomb Shelter

    The last road in Norway and the marker posts on the river, the Borderland Museum, the rock shelter of the 320 raids, and huskies and king crab at the Snowhotel

  • Bergen in Four Hours

    The fortress, Bryggen, the fish market and the funicular up Fløyen — back by 12:15

  • Up Stoltzekleiven, Down Fløyen

    Some 900 stone steps up the mountain and a forest walk down — free, and back by 12:15

  • Grieg's Troldhaugen & Fantoft Stave Church

    By light rail to a stave church in the woods and the composer's villa on the lake

  • Bergen in the Rain

    The city that rains most, done indoors: the medieval museum, Munch, home cooking and cinnamon buns

  • Stavanger Under a Roof

    Sardines, ships, fish soup and an oil rig museum — all around the harbour

  • Hike to Preikestolen (Pulpit Rock)

    Long port days only: ferry and bus, four hours on the trail, and the rock 604 m above the Lysefjord

  • Stegastein, Undredal & a Slow Flåm Day

    The platform over the fjord, goat's cheese in a tiny village and a long lunch — very little walking

  • Kayak the Fjord, Climb to Brekkefossen

    A two-hour morning paddle on the Aurlandsfjord, then a steep climb beside a waterfall

  • Lovatnet & Kjenndalen Glacier

    An emerald lake, a quieter glacier than Briksdal and lunch at the old Loen hotel

  • Geiranger on Foot: Westerås & Storseterfossen

    Up to a mountain farm, behind a waterfall, and lunch on the farm above the fjord

  • Ålesund on a Rainy Day

    Art Nouveau under a roof, the town's fishing story and a long bacalao lunch — all flat and close

  • Oslo in Four Hours

    The fortress, the Nobel hall, The Scream and the Opera roof — back by 12:15

  • Oslo for the Price of a Tram Ticket

    The fortress, the City Hall, Vigeland's sculpture park and the Opera roof — all free

  • Oslo on a Rainy Day: Munch & the National Museum

    Two great museums, a taxi between them and lunch indoors on the waterfront

  • Kristiansand for Free

    The old wooden town, shrimp on the pontoon, the island park and the town beach — back by 12:15

  • Trondheim Under a Roof

    Crown jewels, fish soup and cake, a manor full of instruments and the rock museum

  • Rauma Railway & the Romsdal Gondola

    The train and the gondola do all the climbing: the Troll Wall from the window, the fjord from the top

  • Tromsø on a Shoestring

    The wooden cathedral, the main street, a free Arctic botanic garden and the walk over the bridge

  • Svolvær: Up to the Goat

    Steep stone steps to the view of the Goat pinnacle and the harbour, then the war museum and the ice gallery
