Ready-made days

  • Downtown on Foot

    Junkanoo, Parliament, pirates, rum and the Queen's Staircase — all within 15 minutes of the ship

  • Three Forts & the Fish Fry

    Fort Fincastle, Fort Charlotte, conch salad at Arawak Cay and the marching flamingos

  • Paradise Island by Water Taxi

    Across the harbour to Cabbage Beach, Marina Village and the Cloisters

  • Cable Beach by Jitney

    Three miles of sand for $1.50 each way, and conch fritters on the way home

  • Atlantis Day Pass

    Aquaventure's slides, the marine habitats and Cabbage Beach — timed to beat the queues

  • Blue Lagoon Island

    A cay by boat: hammocks, kayaks and the dolphin encounters

  • The Exuma Swimming Pigs

    A full day by powerboat: iguanas, Pig Beach, Thunderball Grotto and the nurse sharks

  • The West End by Taxi

    Clifton Heritage, Jaws Beach and the Love Beach reef, far from the crowds

  • Two Hours Ashore

    Junkanoo costumes, Parliament and the Straw Market — all flat, all close

  • Nassau on Foot for (Almost) Free

    The old jail library, the cathedral, the Queen's Staircase and the city beach

  • Rainy Day in Nassau

    Three museums, Bay Street's shops and lunch — under a roof, back by 1 PM

  • Pirates & the Beach with Kids

    Blackbeard's museum, straw hats and a swim — the longest walk ten minutes

  • Beaches by Jitney

    The local minibus to two calm public beaches and the Fish Fry

  • Chocolate, Rum & Conch

    A chocolate class, a rum estate and conch salad by the docks, in 4 hours

  • Cabbage Beach in a Morning

    Water taxi to Paradise Island's white sand, pier to pier in 4 hours

  • Marina Village, the Easy Way

    By taxi over the bridge: yachts, paved paths and a long harbour lunch

  • Art & Cathedrals on the Hill

    Government House, the national gallery and two cathedrals behind Bay Street

  • Gardens, a Fort & the East End

    Been here before? Native palms, Nassau's oldest fort and lunch on the harbour

  • Marching Flamingos Morning

    The 10:30 flamingo parade, the quarry garden and fish fry lunch, in 4 hours

  • Clifton & Adelaide Beach

    Heritage park in the morning, the calmest shallow beach in the afternoon

  • Bond Reefs Snorkel

    A snorkel boat from the south-west coast to the film reefs

  • Rose Island by Catamaran

    Powder sand and snorkel reefs on a long, empty cay

  • Rainy Day at Atlantis

    Sharks and rays in the marine habitats, then the marina village's shops
