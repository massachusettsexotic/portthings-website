St. Thomas & St. John

US Virgin Islands

A deep, offline guide to St. Thomas & St. John, inside the Port Things app. Every place shows walking minutes from your pier, and the day plans are timed around the all-aboard. Every place, description and day plan is on the phone, so it works with no signal. Only the map background needs one.

Ready-made days

  • Charlotte Amalie on Foot

    From the Havensight gangway: the Danish fort, Emancipation Garden, the 99 Steps, Blackbeard's Castle, Main Street and the Skyride

  • Mountain Top & Magens Bay

    The island's high viewpoint, Drake's Seat, then an afternoon on its most famous beach

  • St. John by Ferry

    Red Hook to Cruz Bay: Trunk Bay's snorkel trail, the turtles of Maho Bay and the Annaberg ruins

  • Coral World & Coki Point

    The underwater observatory, the island's easiest shore snorkel, and Sapphire Beach

  • The Crown Bay Day: Water Island

    A ten-minute ferry from beside the pier to Honeymoon Beach, then lunch in Frenchtown

  • Jost Van Dyke (Passport Needed)

    Foxy's and the Soggy Dollar Bar in the British Virgin Islands — only on a long port day with a guaranteed return

  • Rainy Day at Havensight

    Pirate museum, children's museum, duty-free and coffee — steps from the ship

  • Rainy Day in Charlotte Amalie

    The fort museum, the warehouse alleys, lunch at Gladys' and the Weibel Museum

  • Charlotte Amalie for Free

    Walk from the pier: the waterfront, churches, the 99 Steps and the old market

  • A Taste of St. Thomas

    Johnnycakes, hot sauce, the old market, ice cream and saltfish at Gladys'

  • Blackbeard's Hill & the Old Houses

    Taxi up, walk down: the watchtower, the great houses, the 99 Steps and the fort

  • Frenchtown from Crown Bay

    The fishing village settled from St. Barths: its little museum, lanes and lunch by the water

  • Planes & Turtles: Lindbergh and Brewers Bay

    Two calm beaches minutes from the Crown Bay pier — back by 1 PM

  • Morningstar Beach, Close to the Ship

    Ten minutes from Havensight: a gentle swim and lunch looking at the harbour

  • Havensight Without a Taxi

    The Skyride up to Paradise Point, the superyachts and duty-free — all flat and close

  • Magens Bay Morning

    The island's famous beach before the crowds, back aboard by 1 PM

  • Quiet East End Beaches

    Undeveloped Lindquist Beach, then the gentle cove at Secret Harbour

  • Drake's Seat & Hull Bay

    The big north-side view, then a fishing-boat beach and lunch under the trees

  • St. John: Trail to Honeymoon Beach

    Ferry to Cruz Bay, a mile over the headland to a quiet bay — long port days

  • Annaberg & Waterlemon Cay

    Sugar-mill ruins, St. John's best snorkel and a shady loop trail — long port days

  • Ram Head & Salt Pond Bay

    St. John's wild south: a clifftop hike, a snorkel and Coral Bay — long port days

  • Snorkel Sail to Buck Island

    A booked boat to the turtles of Turtle Cove and a shipwreck, back by midday
