Ready-made days

  • Valletta: Cathedral, Palace & the Noon Gun

    The whole capital on foot from the gangway — the lift up, Caravaggio, the Knights' palace, the gun at noon and the fort at the tip

  • Three Cities: Birgu, Fort St Angelo & Senglea

    A €1.50 ferry across the harbour to the Knights' first home — the Inquisitor's cells, the maritime museum, the fort and the watchtower with the eye and ear

  • Mdina, Rabat & Dingli Cliffs

    Bus 51 to the Silent City before the coaches, cake on the bastion, Roman catacombs, pastizzi in Rabat and the highest cliffs in Malta

  • Temples, Blue Grotto & Marsaxlokk

    Bus 74 south to the 5,000-year-old temples, the boat into the blue caves, lunch among the luzzu boats and a swim at St Peter's Pool

  • Comino: Blue Lagoon, Crystal Lagoon & the Tower

    The turquoise lagoon before the day boats, the quieter lagoon over the headland, the Knights' tower on the cliffs — and two hours back to the ship

  • Gozo: Ġgantija, the Cittadella & Dwejra

    The fast ferry from the Grand Harbour, the oldest temples on earth, the citadel on the hill, a Gozitan platter and the Inland Sea — back with two hours to spare

  • North Beaches: Għajn Tuffieħa, Golden Bay & Mellieħa

    Bus 44 to the red-gold bay with no road, the sandy beach over the headland, Popeye's village and the longest beach in Malta for the last swim

  • Valletta in Four Hours

    The lift up, Caravaggio, pastizzi and the noon gun — back by 12:30

  • Valletta Under a Roof

    A wet day of palaces and museums, a short hop apart

  • Malta at War: Tunnels & Shelters

    The War Rooms, the George Cross and a rock-cut shelter — mostly underground

  • Mdina & Rabat on a Rainy Day

    Roman mosaics, a nobleman's palace, the cathedral treasures and hot pastizzi

  • The Grand Harbour for a Few Euros

    Free gardens and bastions, the €1 lift and a €1.50 ferry to Birgu and Senglea

  • A Taste of Valletta

    Pastizzi for breakfast, the food market, the noon gun and a long Maltese lunch

  • Marsaxlokk: Boats, Market & Fish Lunch

    The painted luzzu harbour by bus, the Sunday fish market and a long lunch on the quay

  • Sliema to St Julian's by the Sea

    A €1.50 ferry, a flat seafront walk, a swim at Balluta and lunch on Spinola Bay

  • The Harbours by Boat

    Forts and creeks from the water, sitting down — a gentle short-call morning

  • Aquarium, Promenade & a Shallow Beach

    Reef fish in a tunnel, ice cream in Buġibba and Mellieħa's long sandy bay

  • Three Cities with Kids

    A ferry ride, ship models, a hands-on science centre and a 100-ton gun

  • Mosta Dome, Glassblowers & Spitfires

    Short taxi hops across the middle of Malta — the bomb that didn't explode, craft workshops and wartime planes

  • Five Thousand Years Underground

    The Sleeping Lady, the Hypogeum, Tarxien's spirals and the cave of dwarf elephants

  • Gozo Slowly: Salt Pans, Ta' Pinu & Ramla

    A country sanctuary, sea-cut salt pans, Gozitan ftira and an afternoon on the red sand

  • Gozo's Wild South Coast

    Snorkel a fjord-like inlet, hike the Ta' Ċenċ cliffs and swim again at Xlendi

  • Golden Bay to Ġnejna on Foot

    Three wild west-coast bays joined by a watchtower headland and a cliff path

  • Mdina & Dingli at Golden Hour

    For overnight calls: the Silent City after the crowds, sunset on the cliffs, dinner with the view
