Turks and Caicos Islands

A deep, offline guide to Turks & Caicos, inside the Port Things app. Every place shows walking minutes from your pier, and the day plans are timed around the all-aboard. Every place, description and day plan is on the phone, so it works with no signal. Only the map background needs one.

Ready-made days

  • Grace Bay Classic

    The beach, two reefs from shore, the Saltmills and a sunset dinner — the perfect first day

  • Chalk Sound & the South Shore

    The lagoon that doesn't look real, Sapodilla Bay, Taylor Bay and conch at Omar's

  • Long Bay, Leeward & the Cays

    Kites at Long Bay, the iguanas of Little Water Cay and the emptiness of Half Moon Bay

  • Blue Hills & the Wild Northwest

    Conch at the source, the oldest settlement on Provo, Malcolm's Road and the Northwest Point wall

  • North & Middle Caicos by Ferry

    Flamingos, a plantation, the cliffs of Mudjin Harbour and the Conch Bar Caves — the islands' best day

  • Grand Turk & Salt Cay

    A 30-minute flight to the capital: Front Street, the museum, the lighthouse, the wall and the stingrays of Gibbs Cay

  • Cockburn Town & the National Museum

    From the pier: the wreck museum, Front Street's verandas and lunch at an old inn

  • Governor's Beach on Foot

    A free, calm beach 20 minutes' walk from the ship, past the space-capsule monument

  • The Lighthouse & the North End

    The 1852 lighthouse on the cliffs, a quiet shore and Front Street, back by 1 PM

  • Stingrays at Gibbs Cay

    A small boat from town to the rays in knee-deep water, back by 12:30

  • Rainy Day on Grand Turk

    The National Museum, island art, lunch at the inn and the port shops — all indoors

  • Rainy Day in Grace Bay

    Bakery breakfast, a bookshop, galleries, a deli lunch and ice cream

  • Grace Bay for Free

    For Grace Bay guests: the public beach, a shore reef with your own mask, Thursday's fish fry

  • Five Cays Conch Crawl

    Lunch off the fishing boats, a swim at Sapodilla Bay, fritters and rum punch at sunset

  • West Caicos Wall by Boat

    A booked two-tank dive from Turtle Cove, then an easy shore snorkel at Smith's Reef

  • The Wild Northwest

    A 4x4 track to an empty beach at the marine park, then conch in Blue Hills

  • Provo's Hidden History

    Plantation ruins, sailors' rock carvings, a calm swim and lunch off the boats

  • A Slow Grace Bay Day for Two

    A sunrise walk, the quiet Leeward end, sunset on a deck and dinner under the palms

  • North Caicos Beaches by Ferry

    Snorkel three rocky cays, an empty north-shore beach and a blue hole
